# Department-wise Role Workspace v1

Roles covered:
- Sales Person
- Sales Manager
- Receptionist
- Digital Marketing
- Telecalling

Architecture:
- One workspace page.
- Same backend modules.
- Widgets and process buttons change by role.
- Sidebar filtered by role.
- No separate disconnected dashboards.

Sales Person:
- Own leads
- Pending leads
- Follow-ups / reschedule
- Quick enquiry
- OTP/customer validation
- Test drive / booking initiation
- Vehicle status and allotment view
- Field activity shortcut

Sales Manager:
- Team monitoring
- Team leads and follow-ups
- Query answer
- Booking/test drive/allotment monitoring
- QR enquiry data
- Reports and approvals

Receptionist:
- Daily QR generation
- Showroom enquiry capture
- Manual enquiry if customer mobile issue
- Assignment support

Digital Marketing:
- Digital/field/QR lead monitoring
- Follow-up monitoring
- Reassignment support
- Telecalling assignment support
- Lead modification through governance

Telecalling:
- Call customers
- Verify lead details
- Complete enquiry
- Update follow-up with employee accountability
